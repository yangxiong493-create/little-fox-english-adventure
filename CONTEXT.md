# Little Fox English Learning

This context describes the learning language behind a story-driven English adventure for a five-year-old absolute beginner. The child experiences an adventure; learning concepts remain invisible to the child and guide content progression behind the scenes.

## Curriculum Structure

**Stage**:
A mastery band with a distinct listening and communication goal. A Stage is completed through demonstrated understanding, not by age, calendar time, or lesson count.
_Avoid_: Grade, term, unit

**Adventure World**:
A story region whose visible problem gives a group of Missions a shared purpose and setting.
_Avoid_: Course chapter, module

**Mission**:
A five-to-seven-minute story task that produces one visible change in an Adventure World.
_Avoid_: Lesson, test, worksheet

**Play Beat**:
A short interactive moment inside a Mission, normally lasting twenty to sixty seconds and requiring one clear action.
_Avoid_: Question, exercise, quiz

**Story Goal**:
The concrete thing the child is helping a character accomplish, such as feeding a rabbit or repairing a bridge.
_Avoid_: Learning objective when speaking to the child

## Learning

**Learning Item**:
One teachable unit of meaning: a word, useful chunk, command, dialogue turn, or sound pattern.
_Avoid_: Vocabulary item when the item is not a word

**New Item**:
A Learning Item that the child has not yet met through guided meaning-rich exposure.
_Avoid_: New question

**Review Item**:
A previously introduced Learning Item intentionally returned in a different action or scene.
_Avoid_: Wrong answer, mistake item

**Exposure**:
A non-assessed encounter in which voice, animation, context, and consequence make a Learning Item understandable.
_Avoid_: Instruction, explanation

**Mastery Evidence**:
An observed action showing what the child understood or used, including the scene, hint level, and response type.
_Avoid_: Score, grade

**Met**:
The child has experienced a Learning Item with understandable context but has not yet independently identified it.

**Recognized**:
The child can act on a Learning Item without a revealing visual hint in more than one Play Beat.

**Applied**:
The child can use or understand a Learning Item in a changed scene, combination, or guided role-play.

**Retained**:
The child can recognize or apply a Learning Item again after a meaningful delay.
_Avoid_: Mastered forever

**Review Queue**:
The ordered set of Learning Items due to return because of time, weak evidence, or a need for transfer.
_Avoid_: Wrong-answer list

## Child Experience

**Guidance Ladder**:
The ordered support sequence of demonstration, replay, motion cue, spotlight, assisted demonstration, and simplified choice.
_Avoid_: Tutorial popup, written instructions

**Assisted Success**:
A supported completion after uncertainty in which the character demonstrates and finishes with the child, without failure language or lost rewards.
_Avoid_: Auto-answer, failure

**World Change**:
A lasting visible result of a Mission, such as a flower blooming, a bridge repairing, or a character joining the festival.
_Avoid_: Points payout

**Keepsake**:
A non-random visual memory earned from a specific story event.
_Avoid_: Loot, prize box, collectible currency

**Family Quest**:
An optional off-screen action that helps the child reuse one familiar Learning Item with a caregiver.
_Avoid_: Homework
